Selected projects

Embedded systems / time series

Wearable Sleep Monitor

Can wrist movement and heart rate reveal sleep stages? I aligned recordings from 47 participants across 253 nights, trained a temporal convolutional network, and evaluated it on people held out of training.

I also wrote Arduino firmware to transmit timestamped pulse and motion readings over Bluetooth LE.

Arduino Nano 33 IoT · MAX30102 · Keras

Object detection

Bahraini Currency Recognition

Upload a photo or use a camera to identify Bahraini banknotes. My YOLO detector labels each detected note with its denomination and confidence.

On a 44-image test set, the model reached 98.4% mAP@50. The test set contained no coins.

YOLO26s · Roboflow · Streamlit

Image retrieval / team project

SketchMatch

Draw a doodle and search a gallery of 4,050 sketches. Our team used MobileNetV2 embeddings and cosine similarity to retrieve up to 25 visually similar drawings.

I worked on the Streamlit interface for drawing, uploading, and photographing sketches.
